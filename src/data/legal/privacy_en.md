# Tulaa — Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0 · Effective 2025-09-16**

> **Short version:** Your personal data stays strictly on your device. Analytics and crash reports are off by default. With your explicit consent, anonymous crash logs (Sentry) and feature analytics (PostHog) help us improve app stability — never your mood logs, health data, or period entries. No ads. No sale of personal data.

## 1) What We Collect

* **By default:** Nothing leaves your device. Everything remains 100% offline.
* **If you opt in:**
    * **Anonymous feature analytics via PostHog:** Basic technical details and high-level interaction events (e.g., button clicks, feature adoption) to help us understand what works. We never collect personal notes, logs, or identities.
    * **Anonymous crash reports via Sentry:** Diagnostic crash stack traces, OS version, and device model to fix bugs and crashes. These reports never contain your personal health data.

## 2) What Stays on Your Device

All your personal entries remain exclusively on your device:
* Yoga sessions and history
* Water intake logs
* Mood check-ins and notes
* Daily rhythms and reminders
* Period and cycle logs
* Fasting records

This data is completely private to your phone. **Because we do not sync or store this data on remote servers, we cannot recover it for you if you uninstall the app, reset, or lose your device.**

## 3) How We Use Data

We only use opt-in, anonymized technical telemetry to fix crashes and improve product features. We never sell, rent, profile, or monetize your data. We do not run any advertisements.

## 4) Device Permissions

Tulaa only requests permissions necessary for local features:
* **Notifications:** Used strictly if you schedule reminders or rhythms.
* **Zero invasive access:** We never access your contacts, camera, microphone, photo library, or GPS location.

## 5) Third-Party Services

When telemetry is enabled, we work exclusively with privacy-first infrastructure:
* [PostHog](https://posthog.com/privacy) — Opt-in, privacy-centric product analytics.
* [Sentry](https://sentry.io/privacy) — Opt-in, anonymous application crash diagnosis.

## 6) Data Retention

* **Local data:** Stays on your device indefinitely until you manually clear it or uninstall the app.
* **Diagnostics (Opt-in):** Sentry crash logs and PostHog events are automatically purged periodically.

## 7) Security & Encryption

Local app data is sandboxed within your operating system's secure container. We do not create user accounts, collect email addresses, or store remote personal identifiers.

## 8) Children’s Privacy

Tulaa is not intended for children under 16 years of age. Teens aged 16–18 may use the app only with the consent and guidance of a parent or legal guardian.

## 9) Your Privacy Rights & Controls

* **Export:** Export your local data at any time from settings.
* **Delete:** Clear all app data instantly by uninstalling or clearing the app storage.
* **Toggle telemetry:** Turn anonymous analytics and crash logging on or off at any time in settings.

## 10) Policy Updates

If we make material changes to how we handle data, we will provide an in-app notice and prompt you to review and accept the revised policy.
